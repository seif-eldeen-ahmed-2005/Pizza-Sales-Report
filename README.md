# 🍕 Pizza Sales Report & Business Intelligence Dashboard

An interactive Power BI report analyzing a full year of pizza sales transactions (Jan 2015 – Dec 2015) to optimize restaurant operations, menu planning, and revenue streams[cite: 7, 8].

---

## 📊 Project Overview
This project evaluates total sales ($817.86K), order volumes, and customer ordering behaviors to identify top-performing menu items, peak business hours, and category preferences[cite: 7, 8].

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Pages
1. **Best / Worst Sellers:** Identifies top and bottom 5 pizzas based on total sales, order quantity, and order frequency[cite: 7].
2. **Busiest Days & Times:** Analyzes temporal trends to pinpoint peak sales days (weekends/evenings) and seasonal surges (July and January)[cite: 8].
3. **Sales Performance:** Breaks down revenue contribution by pizza category (Classic, Supreme, Veggie, Chicken) and size (Large, Medium, Small)[cite: 8].

## 💡 Key Insights
* **Top Revenue Item:** *The Thai Chicken Pizza* contributes significantly to maximum sales value[cite: 7].
* **Popular Size:** Large-size pizzas drive the vast majority of overall revenue and order volume[cite: 8].
